## 🎛️ About VloomDAW

**VloomDAW** is a fast, modern, pattern-based Digital Audio Workstation built in the spirit of FL Studio—designed from the ground up for beatmakers, producers, and sound designers who want a native, cross-platform workflow without proprietary lock-in.

> 🚧 **Status: Early Development** — We are actively building the core engine and UI. Follow along, test early builds, and help shape the future of open-source music production!

### ✨ Core Workflow Highlights
* **🥁 Channel Rack & Step Sequencer** — Rapidly sketch beats, drum loops, and rhythmic ideas.
* **🎹 Piano Roll** — Expressive MIDI editing designed for speed and musicality.
* **🎞️ Arrangement Playlist** — Flexible pattern, audio, and automation arrangement.
* **🎚️ Real-Time Mixer** — Low-latency routing, metering, and effects processing.
* **⚡ Native Performance** — Powered by modern **C++20** for deterministic, real-time audio performance.
