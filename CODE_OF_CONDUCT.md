# Code of Conduct

Vloom Audio is a community of musicians and developers. We want everyone to
feel welcome, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Assume good intent and ask before judging
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users

Do not:

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, in public or in private
- Use sexualised language or imagery
- Make personal or political attacks
- Publish someone else's private information without permission
- Keep up any behaviour after being asked to stop

## Scope

This applies in all Vloom Audio spaces (repositories, issues, discussions,
chat) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Email **contact@vloom-audio.com** with what happened, where, and links or
screenshots if you have them. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, depending on how
serious and repeated it is.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
